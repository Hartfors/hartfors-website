ABOUT THE COMPANY

Hartfors AB

Hartfors AB is a Swedish company at the forefront of digital innovation, focused on developing forward-thinking digital products, platforms and brands.

We combine technology, creativity and a long-term vision to build meaningful digital experiences.

Our portfolio includes Rebornly, a digital platform for reborn doll artists, sculptors and collectors, and Frondly, a new digital community for plant collectors.
